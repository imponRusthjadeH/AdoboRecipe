Adobong Manok

Filipino Chicken Adobo a classic savory and tangy dish

Ingredients Meat & Marinade

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2–3 pieces bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp sugar (optional, to balance flavor)
  • 1 medium onion, sliced (optional)

For Cooking

  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Extra salt and pepper to taste
  • Steamed rice for serving

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken: Combine chicken, soy sauce, garlic, and black peppercorns in a bowl. Mix well and marinate for at least 30 minutes.

  2. Heat the oil: Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.

  3. Brown the chicken: Remove chicken from the marinade and fry until lightly browned on all sides. Set aside.

  4. Sauté the garlic: In the same pan, sauté the remaining garlic and onion until fragrant.

  5. Simmer: Return the chicken to the pan. Add the reserved marinade, vinegar, water, and bay leaves. Bring to a boil without stirring for a few minutes.

  6. Cook until tender: Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 30–40 minutes or until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.

  7. Adjust the flavor: Taste the sauce and add salt, pepper, or sugar if needed. Simmer for another 2–3 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.

  8. Serve: Serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy your delicious Filipino chicken adobo!
